# 90-DAYS-FORECAST

## Project Overview

This project focuses on generating a 90-day forecasting analysis for post-sales supply chain operations. The goal is to improve spare parts planning, inventory visibility, and operational decision-making by analyzing historical consumption, inventory levels, and demand trends.

The forecast model supports proactive planning strategies to reduce shortages, optimize inventory coverage, and minimize excess or obsolete stock exposure.

---

## Business Problem

Post-sales supply chain operations require accurate forecasting to ensure spare parts availability while controlling inventory costs. Manual forecasting processes are often time-consuming and may not reflect real operational demand.

This project was developed to provide a data-driven forecasting solution capable of supporting:

* Inventory planning
* Demand forecasting
* EOL (End-of-Life) analysis
* Spare parts replenishment
* Operational continuity
